Questions, answered

Helpful details before you enquire.

The answers below explain how this catalogue works. For a bicycle-specific answer, contact the team.

Can I buy a bicycle on this website?

No. This is a catalogue and enquiry website. It does not accept payment, reserve inventory, or create an order. Contact the team for a confirmed recommendation and quotation.

How do I know which size is right?

Use the product page as a starting point, then ask for fit guidance. A final recommendation should take your measurements, riding position, experience, and intended use into account.

Are the specifications and colours guaranteed?

No. Components, colour, weight, fitted equipment, availability, price, taxes, lead time, and delivery options can change. The team will confirm the exact bicycle and terms in writing before any sale is agreed.

Can I ask about a bicycle not shown here?

Yes. Select "I am not sure yet" in the enquiry form and tell us how you plan to ride. The team can advise on the current range and suitable alternatives.

Do you offer support after purchase?

Contact the team for maintenance guidance, warranty questions, and help with your bicycle. The warranty and returns page explains the support process and important safety checks.

What happens to my contact details?

Your details are used to respond to your enquiry. Optional analytics only runs after you make a privacy choice. Read the Privacy and Cookie policies for more information.

Which bicycle suits a beginner?

Metro One is a calm city choice for ages 18+. Nila Step-Through suits riders aged 14–17, while Udaan Junior is designed for ages 8–13. Everyday Electric can suit riders aged 18+ who want assistance on hills or longer routes.

Can girls and boys use the same bicycle models?

Yes. The catalogue starts with age and experience. The final choice depends on height, reach, comfort, and intended use, so girls and boys can choose whichever frame fits safely.
