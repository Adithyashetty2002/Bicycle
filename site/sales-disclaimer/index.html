Catalogue and Sales Disclaimer

What this website can tell you, and what must be confirmed before a sale.

Important: This website helps you explore the bicycles and send an enquiry. It does not take payment or create an order.

1. Catalogue, not checkout

This website is a catalogue and enquiry service. It is not an online store, offer, quotation, reservation, order confirmation, or binding contract. The website does not accept payments and an enquiry, reply, automated acknowledgement, or discussion does not reserve stock or create an obligation to sell or buy.

2. Quotations and contracts

A sale is made only when the operating business issues and the customer accepts a written quotation or order confirmation that states the exact bicycle, fitted equipment, price, taxes, delivery/collection method, lead time, payment terms, and applicable conditions. The final commercial documents take precedence over catalogue material.

3. Specifications and imagery

Photographs are illustrative. They may show optional accessories, prototype components, different sizes, or colours not supplied with the final product. Components, geometry, specifications, materials, weight, colours, finish, software, battery range, and compatible accessories may change without notice where permitted by law. Weight and range figures can vary with size, configuration, terrain, rider, load, maintenance, conditions, and testing method.

4. Price, stock, taxes, and delivery

Any displayed price is indicative unless a dated written quotation says otherwise. Availability, promotions, taxes, delivery charges, duties, assembly, setup, collection, shipping restrictions, financing, and lead time are subject to written confirmation. Applicable consumer rights are not limited by this notice.

5. Fit, suitability, and safety

Product descriptions and size information are general guides, not a substitute for a professional fitting or safety assessment. The rider is responsible for choosing a suitable bicycle, obeying local law, following manufacturer manuals, checking the bicycle before use, using appropriate protective equipment, and keeping the bicycle maintained. Electric bicycles require particular care around charging, storage, transport, and local e-bike rules.

6. Errors and updates

Reasonable effort is made to keep the catalogue accurate. If a material error is identified, the business may correct it, withdraw a model, or decline an enquiry before a contract is formed, subject to applicable law. Nothing here limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
