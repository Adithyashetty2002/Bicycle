Support after purchase

Warranty, Returns, and Safety

Practical guidance for warranty questions, returns, maintenance, and safe riding.

Need support? Send an enquiry with your bicycle model, purchase details, and a clear description of the issue.

Warranty scope

Warranty coverage depends on the exact bicycle and component manufacturer. Your written quotation or order confirmation will identify the warranty provider, covered items, duration, exclusions, proof-of-purchase requirements, and available remedies. Statutory consumer rights continue to apply.

Returns and cancellations

Return and cancellation rights depend on the actual sales channel, whether a product is made to specification, local consumer law, and the business's approved policy. Contact the team promptly with your order details so eligibility, timing, inspection, costs, and the available remedy can be confirmed.

Service and maintenance

Before every ride, check brakes, tyres, fasteners, steering, wheels, lights, and any battery/electrical system according to the manufacturer manual. Have safety-critical work carried out by a competent technician. Normal wear, punctures, damage, misuse, unauthorised modification, accidents, and failure to maintain the bicycle may affect warranty coverage.

Electric bicycle safety

Use only approved chargers, avoid damaged batteries or cables, charge in a dry ventilated location away from combustible materials, follow storage-temperature guidance, and stop using a damaged or overheating battery. Follow local road, helmet, speed, transport, recycling, and battery-disposal rules. Follow all manufacturer safety and recall instructions.

Getting help

Use the contact page for warranty, return, service, or safety questions. Include the model, the issue, and photographs where useful, but never include payment-card details or passwords.
