Right-sized for learning.

Children learn more comfortably on a bicycle they can control. Udaan keeps weight, reach, braking, and gearing appropriate for developing riders.

  • Low standoverMakes stopping and placing both feet down less intimidating.
  • Reachable controlsBrake levers and handlebar dimensions suit smaller hands.
  • Visible and practicalA bright frame, reflectors, and chain protection support everyday safety.

Who it suits

Girls and boys who can balance independently and are developing road-awareness skills.

Where it works

Parks, quiet neighbourhoods, protected cycle paths, and supervised family rides.

Safety first

Use a correctly fitted helmet, closed shoes, visible clothing, and responsible adult supervision.
