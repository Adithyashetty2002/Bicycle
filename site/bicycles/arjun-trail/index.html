Built for the rough line.

Arjun Trail adds suspension, tyre grip, and a wider gear range for riders who want control beyond smooth roads without the weight of a full-suspension bike.

  • Front suspensionAbsorbs repeated impacts and can lock out for smoother roads.
  • Wide climbing rangeLower gears help manage steeper trails and longer ascents.
  • Direct controlA flat bar, strong brakes, and trail tyres keep handling precise.

Who it suits

Riders aged 14 to 17, taller riders, and confident cyclists developing trail skills.

Where it works

Broken roads, forest tracks, rocky paths, climbing routes, and weekend fitness rides.

How it feels

Responsive and planted, with a sportier position than the city-focused models.
