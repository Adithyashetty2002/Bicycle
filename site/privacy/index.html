Privacy Policy

How personal information is handled when you use this catalogue and enquiry service.

Your choice matters: Optional analytics stays off until you accept it, and you can change that choice from Cookie preferences in the footer.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Vayu Bicycles is responsible for information submitted through this catalogue and enquiry website. Privacy questions can be sent through the contact page.

2. Information collected

When you submit an enquiry, the website may collect your name, email address, selected bicycle, enquiry type, and message. The site should not ask for card details, passwords, government identifiers, or sensitive health information.

If you choose analytics, the site may also receive usage information such as pages viewed, device/browser information, approximate location, and interaction events. Analytics events must never include form fields, email addresses, telephone numbers, message content, or stable personal identifiers.

3. Why information is used

Enquiry data is used to answer your request, provide model/fit/availability information, prepare a quotation if requested, prevent abuse, and maintain the service. Analytics is used only to understand site performance and improve the browsing experience.

4. Service providers and transfers

The site may use Netlify for hosting and enquiry forms, Bunny.net for image delivery, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and an email provider. These services process only the information needed for their role and are subject to their own privacy terms.

5. Retention and security

Enquiries should be retained only for the period needed to respond, handle the resulting customer relationship, and meet applicable legal obligations. Access is limited to people who need the information to handle the request.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to withdraw consent for optional analytics. Use the contact page to make a privacy request.

7. Children and changes

This website is not designed for children. Material policy changes should be dated and communicated where required.
