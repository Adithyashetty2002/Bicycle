Privacy controls

Cookie Policy

How this site uses strictly necessary storage and optional analytics technologies.

Your current control: Optional analytics is loaded only after you select Accept. You can reopen your choice from the footer at any time.

Essential preferences

The site stores your analytics choice locally under vayu-analytics-consent. If browser storage is restricted, it uses the first-party vayu_analytics_consent cookie for the same purpose. This preference remembers whether optional analytics may run and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Optional analytics

If you choose to accept analytics, the site may load Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. Google Analytics measures aggregate usage and conversion events. Clarity helps identify usability problems through behavioural analytics. Contact-form fields and other sensitive content must remain masked and must not be transmitted as analytics data.

Consent and withdrawal

Optional analytics is not loaded until you make a choice. You can withdraw or update the choice through the Cookie preferences control in the footer. Where regional law requires it, analytics and advertising storage must default to denied until valid consent is received.

Third-party controls

Browser settings can block or delete cookies, but this may affect how your preference is remembered. You can also reject optional analytics through the control on this site.
