About Vayu

Bicycles should make more of the day possible.

Vayu is built around a simple idea: the bicycle you reach for most should feel clear, capable, and comfortable from the first mile.

For the route in front of you

We focus on purposeful bicycles rather than an endless wall of options. Every model is designed around a distinct use: the daily city run, the route that gets rough around the edges, or the commute that needs a little help.

That means practical details receive the same attention as the frame: reliable braking, comfortable contact points, useful mounts, clear setup advice, and support after the first ride.

Advice before a transaction

The site is intentionally an enquiry service. A product page can help you understand a bicycle, but it cannot replace a conversation about fit, local conditions, availability, and the way you want to ride. We invite you to contact the team for a confirmed recommendation and quotation.

Choose by the ride

Metro One covers everyday city travel, Terrain Works handles mixed surfaces, and Everyday Electric helps on longer or steeper routes. Nila Step-Through makes mounting easier, Arjun Trail supports confident off-road riding, and Udaan Junior gives younger riders child-sized control.
